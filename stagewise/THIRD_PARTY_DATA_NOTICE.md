The public example is a derived stagewise coverage summary from public CISA KEV and historical FIRST EPSS data. Original third-party terms continue to apply. No raw third-party source archive is redistributed in this package.

## Source attribution and license boundary

The MIT license in `LICENSE.txt` covers the project's own software and documentation. It does not replace the terms or copyright notices of third-party sources included in or used by the derived artifacts.

- CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV): the outcome cohort derives from the public catalog, with the exact pinned source identified in the reconstruction provenance. See <https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog> and <https://github.com/cisagov/kev-data>.
- FIRST EPSS: the event accounting and reconstruction use historical EPSS scores. Attribution is retained to FIRST's EPSS Special Interest Group and the score provider; original source terms apply. See <https://www.first.org/epss/> and <https://www.first.org/epss/faq>.
- Shadowserver: historical acquisition URLs and provenance are retained in the EPSS manifests. No Shadowserver host-level telemetry or raw source cache is included. See <https://www.shadowserver.org/>.
- Official CVE records and cvelistV5: publication-state evidence is derived from the official repository at the pinned commit recorded in Supplementary Material 3. The CVE copyright designation, complete terms-of-use license, and disclaimer are reproduced in `THIRD_PARTY_CVE_LICENSE.txt`. Preserve that notice with the reconstruction artifact. See <https://github.com/CVEProject/cvelistV5> and <https://www.cve.org/Legal/TermsOfUse>.

The archived source inputs retain their hashes and source identifiers. Redistribution of this repository does not grant additional rights to external raw data. No raw historical EPSS cache, full CVE repository, or external feasibility dataset is redistributed.
